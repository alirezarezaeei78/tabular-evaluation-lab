# Churn Decision Lab — frozen protocol, 21 October 2026

Declared before any model fit or evaluation inspection. Established methods, one local retrospective benchmark; no scientific novelty or causal retention claim.

Question: how does a separately fitted sigmoid calibrator change probability quality and the number of records flagged by a fixed decision rule? How does that differ from an exact review budget?

Data: UCI Iranian Churn, DOI 10.24432/C5JW3Z, donated 8 April 2020, CC BY 4.0. Provider describes first-nine-month aggregated attributes and a month-12 churn label, with a three-month planning gap. The CSV lacks customer-level observation timestamps; this is NOT a chronological or prospective validation. Use all downloaded rows. Normalize column whitespace. Drop `Status` and derived `Customer Value` conservatively; no IDs or target are predictors. Keep the other eleven numeric features, including Age and Age Group. Do not claim the excluded columns are proven leakage: their derivation/operational availability is not verified here.

Group exact eleven-predictor tuples WITHOUT labels before splitting, including conflicting-label duplicates. Use GroupShuffleSplit: 80/20 development/test, then 75/25 of development into fit/calibration. This is approximately 60/20/20 by groups, not guaranteed by rows or class counts. Seeds 13, 29, 47 are all declared; no rerolls. Require both classes in all partitions or fail. Seed 13 is the primary reported split and the app model, chosen before results. Others are sensitivity runs with overlapping benchmark rows, not three independent validations or confidence intervals. Save row/group mappings and predictions locally; publish aggregate counts/hashes only.

Fixed models: training-prevalence DummyClassifier; StandardScaler + LogisticRegression(C=1, max_iter=2000); HistGradientBoostingClassifier(learning_rate=.06, max_iter=150, max_leaf_nodes=15, l2_regularization=1, early_stopping=False). Train on the fit partition only. No hyperparameter search, class weighting, resampling or feature selection. Apply CalibratedClassifierCV(method='sigmoid', cv='prefit') under scikit-learn 1.4.2 to the SAME fitted boosting model, fitting only calibration-partition labels. Do not refit on evaluation data. Verify base predictions are unchanged by calibration fitting.

Hypothesis (not a promised result): sigmoid calibration may reduce Brier loss; a monotonic mapping can change a fixed 0.5 decision without changing ranking. Report calibration deterioration, slope reversals or numerical ties if observed. Primary: Brier loss and AP on the same held-out rows. Brier combines discrimination and calibration; include reliability-bin means, event rates and supports rather than interpreting it as a pure calibration statistic. Also save log loss, class prevalence, and all policy counts, false positives and misses.

Three prespecified policies for every model: probability >=0.5; a threshold equal to the calibration set's top-ceil(10% * n_cal) cutoff, applied unchanged to evaluation (ties included, review share may drift); and an exact top-ceil(10% * n_eval) capacity selected on evaluation SCORES ONLY. The 10% budget is hypothetical, not measured business staffing. Exact-capacity ties break by stable record ID; cutoff policy includes all ties. Report precision, recall, TP/FP/FN/TN, selected count and share. Never optimize a cutoff on test labels. No cost, revenue or retention gain estimate.

Execution budget: CPU, existing environment, one thread, three compact runs, at most20 minutes classical fitting total. Save versions, hardware description (no machine/user names), every seed/configuration, fit seconds, convergence/stops and stdout. Fit seconds are operational logs, not a controlled latency comparison. Stop on missing source/license, schema/class/finite errors or budget failure.

Checks: exact group disjointness and exhaustive partitions; train-only scaler statistics and unchanged fitted base after calibration; independent tie-aware AP, Brier and confusion-count oracles; finite bounded probabilities; deterministic score/tie ordering, exact capacity and zero/100% edges; no label/ID input leakage; bad CSV/NaN/Inf/range/schema rejection; label-independent selection; JSON/CSV export parity; end-to-end local browser workflow plus keyboard, mobile viewport and displayed-value checks. Tests using synthetic data verify implementation only. Rerunning the same benchmark after viewing its outputs is a reproduction/exploratory reuse, not new independent evidence.

App: local-only loopback server, no account, API key or external inference service. An authored synthetic demo input is the default; it is never presented as a validation dataset. Model scores describe historical churn risk, not who would benefit from an intervention. Real uploaded CSVs remain in local process memory and are not sent to a remote service or saved by the app. Include optional supplied binary outcomes only for descriptive batch errors. No automated offers or customer decisions.

Public release: fresh authored code, MIT examples and original figures, this protocol, source/license ledger, data hashes and aggregate results. Raw UCI input, row-level audit, user uploads and pickle/joblib models stay local and outside the explicit commit allowlist. Reproduction downloads the provider CSV and trains the fixed model. Static figures only for this article; no video creation or upload.
